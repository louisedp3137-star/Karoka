ABOUT

Karoka is led by Louise du Plessis, an experienced English educator with 30 years of teaching across three countries and educational systems.

Her career includes 7 years as a certified South African secondary school English teacher, 14 years as a certified Advanced Skills secondary school English teacher in the United Kingdom, and 9 years teaching middle and secondary school English in the French education system.

This international expertise ensures every student receives instruction tailored to their unique needs and goals.
