ABOUT

Karoka is led by Louise du Plessis, an experienced English educator with 30 years of teaching across three countries and educational systems. Her career includes 7 years as a certified South African secondary school English teacher, 14 years as a certified Advanced Skills secondary school English teacher in the United Kingdom, and 9 years teaching middle and secondary school English in the French education system. This international expertise ensures every student receives instruction tailored to their unique needs and goals.

Educational Qualifications

  • Specialist Leader in Education, UK (2013)
  • Chartered London Teacher, UK (2009)
  • Mentoring Training, University of London (2008)
  • Advanced Skills Teacher, UK (2006)
  • Leading Practitioner, UK (2002)
  • Qualified Teacher Status, UK (2001) / SA (1993)

Core Competences

  • CEFR-aligned Lesson Planning and Delivery to CEFR level A1 to C2
  • IELTS and other Language Proficiency Assessment Preparation

  • Educational Consultancy and Programme Coordination
