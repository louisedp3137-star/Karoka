SERVICES

English Tutoring

Personalised English tutoring to help learners communicate clearly, confidently, and accurately. All courses are aligned with the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages).

Includes:

  • One-to-one English lessons
  • Pair and small-group English classes
  • CEFR level progression (A2+, B1, B2, C1, C2)
  • Exam and certification prep (IELTS, Cambridge English, Pearson PTE, Ev@lang, French Baccalaureate, CNED)
  • Business English and service-industry English
  • Classroom, homeschooling, and online lessons

Each programme is fully personalised following a level assessment and consultation.

Educational Consultancy

Professional educational consultancy services for schools, NGOs, and institutions in Madagascar and beyond.

Includes:

  • English curriculum development
  • Teacher training and pedagogy support
  • School management consultancy
  • Academic and professional proofreading & editing

Consultancy projects are designed around your institutional context and objectives.
