SERVICES

English Tutoring

Karoka offers personalised English tutoring programmes designed to help learners communicate clearly, confidently, and accurately in English. All courses are aligned with the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) to ensure measurable progress.

English tutoring services include:

  • One-to-one English lessons
  • Pair and small-group English classes
  • CEFR level progression (A2+, B1, B2, C1, C2)
  • Exam and certification preparation (IELTS, Cambridge English, Pearson PTE, Ev@lang, French Baccalaureate, CNED)
  • Business English and service-industry English
  • Classroom teaching, homeschooling, and online English lessons

Each programme is fully personalised following a level assessment and consultation.

Educational Consultancy Services

In addition to tutoring, Karoka provides professional educational consultancy services for schools, NGOs, and institutions in Madagascar and beyond.

Consultancy services include:

  • English curriculum development
  • Teacher training and pedagogy support
  • School management consultancy
  • Academic and professional proofreading & editing

Consultancy projects are designed around your institutional context and objectives.
